Hiring Your First Helper

You're one person running a small business, and you've hit the moment where one more pair of hands would genuinely help. This manual walks the whole arc in plain English: deciding what to hire, writing the posting, paid trial projects, paying people on time, keeping just enough records, onboarding without info-flooding, kind offboarding — and staying sane about all of it.

Education only, never legal or tax advice. This guide explains concepts so you can have better conversations with professionals (an accountant, a lawyer, your state's business office); it does not replace them.
