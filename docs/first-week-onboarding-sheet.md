# First-Week Onboarding Sheet

Fill this in **before** your helper's first day, then send/adopt it during
Stage 2 of onboarding (Ch. 6). One primary task, one bit of context, one
touchpoint per day — deliberately light. "Email/Slack" below means whichever
channel you actually check.

## Rules of the week (send as-is with the sheet)

- One rule if stuck: if you're blocked more than 20 minutes, message me. It
  is genuinely no bother.
- One rule about hours: I don't expect replies from you outside [these hours],
  and I mean it.
- One rule about done: "done, plain, on time" beats "perfect, late."
